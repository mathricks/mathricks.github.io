Common Questions

What does the app cost?

Recording is free. The handover to your tax adviser is unlocked with a one-off purchase per tax year, from tax year 2026 on. No subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap “Restore purchases” in the Reports or Property tab. A purchase covers one tax year and belongs to your Apple Account.

How do I back up my data?

In the Property tab, turn on “iCloud backup”, or “Create backup file” and keep the file yourself. The same place restores your data or deletes the backup.

Before Sending

Please include your iOS version, device model, app version, selected language, and a short description of what happened.

The app does not provide tax advice. For tax decisions, consult your tax adviser.

Please do not send tax identification numbers, complete tax returns, unredacted receipts or guest data. A redacted example is usually enough to investigate a technical issue.
