The app

Record it while it is still fresh.

Use

Rented out, used by you or vacant – day by day in a calendar.

Evidence

Receipts and photos kept where they belong.

Handover

An annual report for your tax adviser.

Recording is free. The handover is unlocked with a one-off purchase per tax year. No subscription.

Privacy first

No account, and no data with Mathricks.

On your device

Your data stays on your iPhone or iPad. No ads, no tracking. A backup to your own iCloud is optional.

Not tax advice

Ferienwohnung documents use. It does not prepare a tax return and does not replace tax advice.
