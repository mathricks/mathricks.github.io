Usage calendar

Every day of the year, classified while it is still fresh.

Tap a day, pick the type of use and adjust From and To. The calendar fills with colour, and at the end of the year there is nothing left to piece together from booking platforms, invoices and memory.

  • Rented out
  • Own use
  • Family / friends
  • Reserved for own use
  • Maintenance, cleaning, handover
  • Available to rent
  • Vacant
  • Not available

Record periods

A period has a type of use, a start and an end. Every let carries its platform and booking reference, and every maintenance visit its reason: final cleaning, key handover, repair, renovation, inspection or servicing.

Import calendar files

Read in the calendar file (.ics) from your booking platform. The periods found are shown as proposals and are only saved once you confirm them.

Evidence with the period

Booking statements, payout statements, contractor invoices and photos sit with the period they belong to – not in a folder nobody can sort out at the end of the year.

Anlage V-FeWo

The day counts the form asks for – and how each was reached.

For every holiday home, the German tax form Anlage V-FeWo asks for the days of own use, the rental days, the vacant days and the local customary rental days. Ferienwohnung groups your types of use the same way and shows what each figure is made of.

Days of own use

Reserved days count here too, whether or not anyone went.

  • Own use
  • Family / friends
  • Reserved for own use

Rental days

Every day let, with platform, booking reference and evidence.

  • Rented out

Vacant days

Maintenance visits count here and are listed separately in the report, with their reason.

  • Maintenance, cleaning, handover
  • Available to rent
  • Vacant
  • Not available

Local customary rental days

This figure does not come from your calendar. Enter it with its source, such as the annual statistics of the local tourist board, and it appears in the report next to your own rental days.

The grouping is a proposal for review. How a day is treated for tax purposes is for your tax adviser to decide.

Sample report

What the usage file looks like as a PDF.

The preview shows the first page of an annual report with fictional data, created in German. The report follows the language you choose in the app. The full report contains:

  • An overview of every type of use in the year
  • The Anlage V-FeWo figures and how they were reached
  • Open items, stated rather than left out
  • The chronological usage history, including maintenance visits
  • References to the evidence on file

Every version is kept in the report history, and the app shows when data has changed since the last report.

Privacy first

No account, and no data with Mathricks.

Stored on your device

Properties, usage periods, evidence and reports live in the app's protected storage on your iPhone or iPad. The app works offline, with no ads and no tracking. A backup to your own iCloud is optional: it stays off until you turn it on, and you can switch it off and delete it at any time.

A record, not tax advice

Ferienwohnung documents actual use. It does not prepare a tax return, sends nothing to the tax office and does not replace tax advice.
