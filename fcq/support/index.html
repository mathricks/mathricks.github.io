Frequently Asked Questions

Can the app actually factor an RSA number?

It runs genuine factoring attempts, but random trial division will not factor a modern RSA challenge number. The odds shown in the app are real, and they are astronomically small. It's a benchmark and a way to feel how hard factoring is.

Is there a prize?

No. RSA Laboratories ended the Factoring Challenge in 2007, and no prize has been available since. The original prize amounts are shown for history only.

Why is a target marked factored when I didn't solve it?

The app keeps its challenge list current. When a new factorization is published and verified, the target's status, factors and solve date update on the next daily refresh, with no app update needed.

Where is my progress stored?

Tested and drawn totals are saved per target on your device only. They are not synced between devices and are removed if you delete the app.

What does "Chance to Find" mean?

It's the probability that the candidates you've tested so far include the smaller prime P, given how many candidates the search range holds. Tap any term marked with ⓘ in the app for a short explanation.

Can I test my own prime?

Yes. Enter it in Prime Controls. It must lie inside the search range for the selected target, between the "From" and "Through" bounds shown below the field.

Does it drain my battery?

Searching is CPU-intensive, like a benchmark. It only runs while the app is open and you have started it, so stop a continuous run when you're done.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Factoring Challenge Quest is completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Troubleshooting

  • Prime is rejected: The number must be prime and inside the search range. Tap "Use" next to a bound, then edit its last digits; the bounds themselves are not prime.
  • Challenge status looks out of date: The list refreshes at most once a day when you're online. Reopen the app later, or compare with the RSA Archive.
  • Speed drops during long runs: iOS may throttle the CPU when the device warms up. Let it cool down, remove heavy cases, or unplug from the charger.
  • Repeated crashes: Restart your device, make sure iOS is up to date, and relaunch the app.
