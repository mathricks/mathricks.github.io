Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Factoring Challenge Quest application for iPhone and iPad ("the app"). The app is provided free of charge by Mathricks and is intended for use "as is".

Information Collection

The app does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no registration, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your factoring statistics, such as the number of candidates tested and drawn for each RSA challenge target, and your preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is never uploaded, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Network Access

At most once a day, the app downloads two small public files from mathricks.com: the list of RSA challenge numbers, so that newly factored targets show up without an app update, and a short notice about Bitcoin Puzzle Quest, another Mathricks app. These requests send no personal data, identifiers or statistics from the app. As with any web request, the hosting provider may briefly process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the files. The app works fully offline using its built-in copies.

App Store Links

If you choose to view Bitcoin Puzzle Quest from within the app, its App Store page is shown by Apple. Any data handled there is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Location Data

The app does not access your location.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Because the app does not collect personal information, no such data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after any update is considered acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the app, contact us at info [at] mathricks [dot] com or via the support page.
