Version 3.2 Latest Release
August 11, 2026

Summit Numbers Pro is here: unlock no ads, unlimited daily games, Hard and Expert puzzles, unlimited saved boards, and 50 welcome Merge Tokens.

This update also adds optional rewarded ads for extra games, clearer Daily Puzzle prompts, Hot Start leaderboards, and visual/gameplay polish throughout the app.

Version 3.1
May 26, 2026

Daily Puzzles are here!

Take on a new set of move-limited challenges inspired by chess brainteasers. Plan carefully, solve each puzzle, unlock the next one and earn a Merge Token the first time you complete each challenge.

This update also includes polish and small improvements across the game.

Version 3.0
May 7, 2026

Version 3.0 introduces "Puzzles", a new way to play Summit Numbers.

Take on move-limited challenges, track your progress and best times in Puzzle Quest, unlock the next puzzle as you go, and earn a Merge Token the first time you solve each one.

We’ve also polished the overall experience.

Version 2.0
March 9, 2026

This big update introduces Multiplayer mode. Play vs Computer or challenge a nearby player with a new lobby, clearer turn indicators, rematch support, and improved board orientation.

Bug fixes and stability improvements included.

Version 1.0 Initial Launch
March 4, 2026

Initial release of Summit Numbers on the Apple App Store.

  • Wrap-Around Mechanics: Move numbers across grid boundaries to combine values and build summits.
  • Tactical Balancing: Balance target pressures and maintain winning streaks through careful planning.
  • Shareable Challenges: Share specific climb configurations with friends to test identical boards.
