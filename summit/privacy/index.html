Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Mathricks, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information in Summit Numbers and on the Summit Numbers pages of the Mathricks website.

Data Collection

Summit Numbers is designed to be privacy-conscious. We do not require an account and we do not directly collect names, email addresses, payment card details, or similar personally identifiable information from players.

  • No Account Required: You can play the game without creating an account.
  • Local Storage: Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Game Center: Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. If you choose to sign in, scores are shared with Apple.
  • In-App Purchases: Purchases and restores are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Subscriptions: If you subscribe to Summit Numbers Pro, your subscription status is managed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not collect or store your identity or payment information. See our Terms of Use for subscription details.

Advertising and Consent

The free version of Summit Numbers may show ads served by Google AdMob. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to support personalized or non-personalized advertising choices.

Google's advertising SDKs may process data such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising data, ad interactions, diagnostics, and performance data. This information may be used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, improve reliability, and support analytics related to advertising.

Where required, Summit Numbers asks for consent before using data for personalized ads. On iOS, the app may also ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before accessing Apple's advertising identifier. If you do not allow tracking or do not consent to personalized ads, you can still use the app and ads may be shown in a non-personalized or contextual form. Summit Numbers Pro subscribers do not see ads while their Pro access is active.

Website Analytics

The Mathricks website may use analytics and click-measurement tools to understand page visits, app download link clicks, and site performance. These tools are separate from the Summit Numbers app and are controlled through the website's consent choices where applicable.

Your Choices

  • You can manage iOS tracking permission in the Settings app.
  • Where a Google consent message is available, you can use it to review or change your advertising choices.
  • You can contact us to ask questions about this policy or your privacy choices.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at info [at] mathricks [dot] com or leave us a message via our support page.
