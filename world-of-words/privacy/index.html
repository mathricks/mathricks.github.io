Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Mathricks, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in World of Words.

Data Collection

World of Words is designed as a privacy-first, offline-first learning app. The app does not collect personally identifiable information.

  • No Account Required: You can use the app without registering or signing in.
  • Local Progress: Discoveries, daily completions, streaks, and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Offline Vocabulary: The current vocabulary and translation pack is bundled with the app. Searches are resolved on-device and are not sent to a translation service.
  • Pronunciation: Spoken pronunciations use Apple speech voices installed or available on your device. Mathricks does not receive the words you play.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The app contains no third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking SDKs, or tracking cookies.

Optional Support, Word, and Audio Requests

Searches and playback stay on your device. If you choose a word-request or audio-report link, your browser opens a separate form on mathricks.com. The app may put the relevant word, language, locale, audio source, report reason, and source=app in that page’s address so that you do not have to re-enter them. Nothing is submitted until you press the form’s button.

A word request stores the word or phrase, any context you choose to provide, an optional email address, the request source, and the submission time. We use it only to review vocabulary requests and, if you provide an email address, to contact you about that request. Word requests are automatically deleted after no more than 180 days.

An audio report stores the reported word, language, locale, reason, audio source, any details you provide, the request source, and the submission time. You may optionally include your email address, a preferred credit name, and a recording of your own voice. A recording is kept in private object storage and is not published automatically. If it may be suitable for the app, Mathricks will contact you to confirm publication permission and credit before using it. Audio reports and recordings are scheduled for automatic deletion once they are 30 days old unless separate permission and arrangements are agreed with you.

Cloudflare provides the forms’ security checks, processing infrastructure, database, and private recording storage. Network information is used transiently for abuse prevention; Mathricks does not store your IP address or user-agent string with a request.

Data Sharing

Because the app does not collect personal data, Mathricks does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

World of Words does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its core learning experience works without an account, advertising, or online profile.

Future Features

If a future version adds an online translation service or another feature that changes data handling, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info [at] mathricks [dot] com or use the World of Words support page.
