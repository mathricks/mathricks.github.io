Help us sound right

Report a pronunciation

Tell us when a word is silent or sounds wrong. Native speakers can also send a short recording for possible credit in a future update.

What happened?

If known, use a language or regional code such as de-CH.

Native-speaker contribution

Optionally attach one clear recording of this word or phrase. M4A, WebM, Ogg, or WAV; maximum 5 MB.

Only send your own voice. Recordings are reviewed privately and are never published automatically.

For a report alone, email is optional. With a recording, we need it to confirm any future use and credit.

Only the fields you submit are sent. Reports and recordings are scheduled for automatic deletion once they are 30 days old. See our privacy policy.

♫

What happens next?

  1. We check the report against the exact word, language, and audio source.
  2. Any recording stays private while it is manually reviewed.
  3. If it may suit the app, we contact you to confirm permission and credit before publication.
