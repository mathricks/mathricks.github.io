How it works

From one search to a whole world of language.

Explore everyday vocabulary, useful travel expressions, many writing systems, and the places where each language is spoken.

Find a concept

Search 1,000 A1 words and phrases, from food, family, and daily routines to directions, transport, shopping, and emergencies.

Explore the globe

Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, and find translations pinned directly to 195 illustrated countries, surrounded by clay-style landmarks.

Tap and listen

Open a translation to see its country, language, and romanization when helpful. Hear accurate recorded or device pronunciation when available.

Explore it. Then play it.

Turn curiosity into recall.

▶

World Sprint

See a translation and type the English word before time runs out. Play an open two-minute sprint, use letter and world hints, and build combo streaks as you go.

◆

Daily Sprint

Take one three-minute run built from the same words for everyone that day. Share a spoiler-free flag grid and optionally join the Game Center leaderboard.

♨

Your passport

Track concepts explored, countries reached, translations found, streaks, and daily sprint history. Set an optional reminder to keep your journey moving.

Made to travel

Keep the world with you, even when the network cannot.

The full vocabulary catalog, globe, and your progress live on your device. Download pronunciation packs by language for offline listening, manage their storage at any time, and use the core app without an account, ads, or tracking.
