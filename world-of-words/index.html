How it works

A world of language in three simple gestures.

Every session begins with a familiar word and turns it into a small journey across countries, scripts, and sounds.

Choose a word

Start with the shared word of the day or explore a curated everyday word such as love, peace, friend, water, or home.

Explore the globe

Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, and follow colorful translations placed directly around the interactive 3D world.

Tap and listen

Open a translation to see its country and language, then hear the pronunciation when a matching device voice is available.

Made for discovery

Playful enough to invite curiosity. Focused enough to build a habit.

◎

Find five translations to complete the daily challenge, grow your streak, and turn a few minutes of exploration into steady practice.
