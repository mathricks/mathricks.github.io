The World of Words

Support Center

Answers to common questions about the current app, plus a direct line for feedback, accessibility notes, and bug reports.

Which words are currently supported?

The current catalog contains 1,000 A1 learning concepts: 907 standalone words and 93 contextual travel expressions, with common aliases recognized in search.

How do I explore translations?

Drag the globe to rotate it, pinch to zoom, and tap a translation. Tap the selected translation again to close it.

Why does a translation sometimes have no audio?

The app uses verified recorded audio where available and can fall back to a matching Apple speech voice. You can also download pronunciation packs by language for offline listening. If no accurate option is available, the app stays silent rather than teaching the wrong sound.

Where is my progress stored?

Discoveries, daily completions, and streak information are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account.

What is the difference between World Sprint and Daily Sprint?

World Sprint is a replayable two-minute vocabulary challenge. Daily Sprint is one shared three-minute challenge per day, with optional Game Center scores and spoiler-free result sharing.

Can I request a word or report a pronunciation?

Yes. The app can open our separate word-request and audio-report forms with the relevant details filled in. Nothing is sent until you choose to submit the form.
