A Faster Hunt: Bitcoin Puzzle Quest 4.0

A good hunt makes you want to keep going. For version 4.0 of Bitcoin Puzzle Quest, we focused on making that time count: a faster on-device scanner, a clearer Power Surge experience, and another side quest for a change of pace.

In our comparison tests, the rebuilt scanner processed around 50 times more candidate keys per second than version 3.0.

A faster hunt on the same device

Version 4.0 reworks the scanning engine and reduces overhead throughout the scanning pipeline. The improvement is most visible in Continuous Mode: the counter advances faster while you follow the results and explore your chosen puzzle range.

You still control how you search. Choose Random or Sequential, enter a starting key, adjust the step size, or narrow the range. During Continuous Mode, the 1–100 control changes how many result rows are shown without limiting scanning speed.

The measured results

We compared versions 3.0 and 4.0 on an iPad Pro (M1) using the same address-target workload. The following physical-device measurements were taken from optimized Release builds.

From version 2.4 to 4.0

To compare all three generations, we also ran versions 2.4, 3.0 and 4.0 in the same optimized Release process on an iPhone 17e simulator. These simulator measurements are separate from the physical iPad results above.

In this matched comparison, version 3.0 was around twice as fast as 2.4, and version 4.0 was around 54 times as fast as 3.0: roughly 100 times the throughput of version 2.4 overall. Simulator rates should not be read as physical-device performance.

Actual throughput varies with device, search settings, temperature and system activity. Scanning runs while the app is open in the foreground. This update does not add background scanning, and faster processing does not imply unchanged battery use.

Speed has to come with correct results

A faster counter is useful only if the search remains correct. We compared the rebuilt engine with our reference implementation and checked known puzzle solutions, matching results, and search controls.

Candidate key generation and matching continue to happen locally and work offline. Candidate private keys and found matches are not uploaded to Mathricks. Optional features such as Game Center, invites, purchase verification and advertising use network services, as described in our privacy policy.

The remaining unsolved puzzle ranges are enormous. Testing more candidates per second does not make an individual candidate more likely to match, and it does not guarantee a discovery or a Bitcoin reward.

More ways to keep questing

The refreshed Power Surge selector offers six packs, from 500 million to 100 billion keys, with a separate confirmation button showing the selected pack and price. The permanent Continuous Mode Unlock remains a one-time purchase. Existing owners keep their entitlement at no extra charge.

For a break from the main hunt, tap the treasure chest for the sliding puzzle or press and hold for Node Sync. Both share the daily chest allowance, and a qualifying win grants 5 million Power Surge keys.

Version 4.0 also refines the range controls, low-key balance display and found-key highlights. Read the full changelog for the release details.

Your next run

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest is free on iPhone and iPad. Try Continuous Mode with earned Power Surge keys, top up with a pack, or use the permanent unlock if you enjoy a longer, hands-free hunt.
