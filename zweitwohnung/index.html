Daily Records

A calendar for the events that matter at tax time.

Tap a date and record what happened: arrival, full day, departure, a trip home, a return trip to the second home, provided meals, ticket costs, and notes.

Calendar entries

Each day can hold meal allowance state, travel direction, optional purchased travel cost, provided meals, and a note.

Expense categories

Track accommodation, furnishings, moving costs, travel receipts, and other work-related expenses in a separate expenses view.

Address setup

Save main-home and second-home addresses, then use Apple MapKit route lookup to estimate the one-way distance.

Annual Overview

Turn scattered records into a reviewable yearly estimate.

The result view brings meal allowances, family trips home, accommodation caps, recorded costs, and plausibility checks into one place before you review everything for your tax return.

Configure assumptions

Default rates for meal allowances, family-trip distance, and accommodation cap are visible and editable.

Review checks

The app flags missing distance, missing three-month meal period setup, and extra trips in the same calendar week.

Export the year

Share all calendar entries and expenses as CSV for Numbers, Excel, or another tax workflow.

Privacy First

Stored on device

Your calendar records, expenses, settings, language, and appearance preference are stored locally on your iPhone.

Organization aid

Zweitwohnung helps organize inputs and estimates. It does not replace tax advice, official forms, or a review of your personal circumstances.
