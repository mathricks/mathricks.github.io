Version 2.0 Latest Release
September 13, 2026

Version 2.0 is our biggest update yet, making it even easier to keep complete supporting records for your work-related second home.

  • New Year Calendar: Shows your entire tax year at a glance. Review unfinished days, edit multiple entries together, and keep different periods, homes, and work locations organized.
  • Expanded Records & Export: Supporting documentation for your second household tax return has been significantly expanded with clearer review checks, receipt status tracking, archived PDF reports, and improved PDF and CSV exports.
  • iCloud Backup: Optionally back up your data, receipts, and reports to your own iCloud and restore them on a new device.
  • Permanent Tax Year Unlock: New tax years can be unlocked once and kept permanently without a subscription.
  • System Optimizations: Numerous improvements to calculations, usability, accessibility, and stability.
Version 1.0 Initial Release
August 18, 2026

Initial release of Zweitwohnung on the Apple App Store.

  • Calendar Records: Easily log daily presence for your work second home: arrival days, full presence days, and departures.
  • Travel & Trips Home: Track eligible weekly family trips home and return journeys with automatic mileage estimation.
  • Expense Tracking: Record rent, utility costs, furnishings, moving expenses, and travel receipts across custom categories.
  • Meal Allowances & Review Checks: Apply statutory three-month meal allowance rules and plausibility checks for tax filings.
  • Tax Summary & Export: Generate clean annual PDF deduction estimates for your tax return and export raw data to CSV for Numbers or Excel.
  • Privacy-First: No account required. All records and receipt copies are stored securely on your device.
