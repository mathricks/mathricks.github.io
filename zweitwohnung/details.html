Feature Details

Structured records for work-related second-home costs in Germany.

Zweitwohnung is built around four app areas: Days, Expenses, Result, and Settings. The app keeps the recordkeeping workflow simple while leaving the underlying assumptions visible and editable.

Record Types

Days
Arrival, full day, departure, no allowance, provided meals, travel direction, ticket cost, notes.
Calendar
Trips
Trips to the main home or second home, plus optional one-way or round-trip travel receipts.
Daily
Expenses
Accommodation, furnishings, moving, travel receipts, and other work-related expenses.
Receipts
Settings
Tax periods, period addresses and distances, meal rates, annual mileage rules, accommodation cap.
Editable

Estimate Inputs

  • Meal allowances default to 14 EUR for arrival and departure days and 28 EUR for full absence days.
  • Meal reductions are applied when breakfast, lunch, or dinner was provided.
  • The three-month meal allowance window can be configured during onboarding or later in the calendar.
  • Family trips home use at most one eligible trip per calendar week and statutory rates for the tax year.
  • Accommodation applies an editable monthly cap to recorded accommodation expenses.
  • Purchased travel receipts are recorded and shown separately for annual comparison.
Workflow

From first setup to annual tax return files.

Guided setup

The onboarding explains the three-month meal allowance period, the basic record types, and the one-tap calendar workflow.

Automatic checks

The result screen flags missing distance, missing meal-period setup, and multiple home trips in the same calendar week.

PDF and CSV export

Create a readable annual PDF report for your tax return, or export raw CSV data for spreadsheet tools and other preparation workflows.

Boundaries

Clear records for private tax returns.

Configurable defaults

The app applies documented standard rules to the information you enter. Addresses and distances remain visible per tax period.

Review required

The app estimates potentially deductible expenses, not a refund. It does not provide tax advice or verify every legal condition. Employer reimbursements, company cars, international cases, and personal circumstances must be checked before filing.
