Version 2.0

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest 2.0: The Big Rebuild!

This version is our biggest update yet, packed with upgrades and new ways to play:

  • Refreshed UI: Sleek glass look with new segmented tabs that can be minimized.
  • Continuous Mode: Run nonstop with Power Surge credits or unlock permanent access.
  • Daily Chest Mini-Game: Solve a quick 3×3 puzzle for bonus credits each day by double-tapping the chest.
