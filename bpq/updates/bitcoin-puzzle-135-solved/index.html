Bitcoin Puzzle 135
Solved and Verified

Bitcoin Puzzle 135 was solved on 28 July 2026. The result was independently verified from the confirmed transaction that swept the address’s 13.5 BTC balance and from the subsequently published private key.

Published 29 July 2026 Last updated 30 July 2026

Announcement

The result is confirmed.

The Puzzle 135 spending transaction 817535…a812 was confirmed in Bitcoin block 959946 at 08:20:16 UTC on 28 July 2026. It spent the three known funding outputs for 16RGFo…w2v, totaling 13.5 BTC.

The solver, RetiredCoder, publicly announced the solve at 08:39:40 UTC and described a five-month search using approximately 200 GPUs. At 20:13:35 UTC on 29 July 2026, RetiredCoder published the Puzzle 135 private key.

What could be verified

We checked the confirmed transaction, block height and block timestamp; traced its inputs to the known Puzzle 135 funding outputs; confirmed the total input and destination amount; and checked the public key disclosed by the spending signatures against the puzzle address. We then derived the compressed secp256k1 public key from the published private key and obtained an exact match: 02145d2611c823a396ef6712ce0f712f09b9b4f3135e3e0aa3230fb9b6d08d1e16. That public key derives the known Puzzle 135 address, 16RGFo6hjq9ym6Pj7N5H7L1NR1rVPJyw2v. Together, these checks independently confirm the published key, control of the address, and the reward claim.
