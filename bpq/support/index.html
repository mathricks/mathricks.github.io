Third-Party Puzzle Disclaimer


Bitcoin Puzzle Quest references the “~1000 BTC Bitcoin Challenge,” a cryptographic puzzle published January 15, 2015 by an anonymous contributor to demonstrate the vastness of Bitcoin's private-key space. The original challenge funded a series of 256 addresses (now 160 active ranges) with a prize pool close to 1000 BTC.

This app provides only a local, offline tool to generate and test private keys to match against that publicly-available list. The developer:
  • Is not affiliated with the puzzle's creator
  • Does not control, sponsor, or administer any prizes or funds
  • Does not store or broadcast keys or transactions
Any Bitcoin discovered belongs solely to the private-key holder. Apple Inc. is not a sponsor or organizer of this challenge.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Bitcoin Puzzle Quest?
Bitcoin Puzzle Quest (BPQ for short) is a puzzle-hunting app that lets you generate random (or sequential) keys and test and match them against the famous Bitcoin Puzzle address list - entirely offline, on your own iOS device.
What is a Bitcoin Puzzle, or BTC Puzzle?New
A Bitcoin puzzle is a public cryptographic challenge in which bitcoin was placed at addresses whose private keys were deliberately chosen from limited, known ranges. Solving one means finding the private key for a funded puzzle address; it does not mean that Bitcoin itself has been broken. Read our plain-English guide to the 1000 BTC Challenge.
How are the puzzle levels structured?
The challenge is divided into 160 levels (originally 256), with each level using a key range that is twice as large as the previous one. For example:
  • Level 1:      2⁰ ≤ key < 2¹ (only 1 possible key)
  • Level 2:      2¹ ≤ key < 2²
  • Level 3:      2² ≤ key < 2³
  • ...
  • Level 160:    2¹⁵⁹ ≤ key < 2¹⁶⁰
Each higher level doubles the size of the key space, making it exponentially harder to solve. To reflect this, the puzzle creator(s) placed increasing amounts of Bitcoin in higher levels as added incentive.
How do I start playing Bitcoin Puzzle Quest?
Open the app and start testing keys by choosing your difficulty level and number of tries and hit the 'Go' button. Quest Overview also gives you a 3D map of the whole puzzle to jump in from (see below).
What is the Quest Overview (3D view)?
The Quest Overview is a 3D map of all puzzle ranges, added in version 2.2. At a glance you can see which puzzles are already solved, which have been tried, and which are still untouched, then tap a range to jump straight into your next hunt.
What is Power Surge?
Power Surge is our reward system that gives you temporary access to Continuous Mode, the auto-tapping, sequential-scanning fast lane, without a purchase. You earn free Power Surge keys two ways: by solving the Daily Chest mini-game and by inviting friends who go on to qualify. When you earn or spend them you'll see a quick reward toast confirming it.
How do I invite friends and earn free Power Surge keys?
Open the menu and choose Invite Friends. Once you're signed in to Game Center, BPQ shows your personal invite code and share link. Send that code or link to a friend; they can install Bitcoin Puzzle Quest, open Invite Friends, and redeem it from the same screen.

After your friend starts playing and tests at least 10,000 keys, both of you receive 1,000,000 promotional Power Surge keys. These are in-game bonus keys for Continuous Mode, not BTC, cash, or a financial reward. Invite codes are tied to Game Center profiles, you cannot redeem your own code, and eligibility checks or reward limits may apply. No names, emails, or contacts are collected or uploaded.
What does “difficulty level” mean?
The difficulty level defines the bit-size, i.e. the bit-length of the secret key. The 26-bit puzzle contains 225 possible keys (≈33 million); the 160-bit puzzle, the "beast", contains 2159 (≈7.3 x 1047). Bigger bits = bigger needle in the haystack.
Are there in-app purchases?
Yes, and they're all optional. The core game is free, and you can also earn Continuous Mode for free via Power Surge. There are two kinds of purchase:
  • Million-Key Packs (consumable): 10M, 50M, 100M or 250M key packs. These top up your Continuous Mode key allowance.
  • Continuous Mode Unlock A one-time purchase for permanent, unlimited Continuous and sequential scanning.
Prices are set on App Store and may vary by region. There are no subscriptions.
Are there ads?New
Yes. The free version may show Google AdMob banner ads and occasional full-screen ad breaks. It may also offer an optional rewarded ad for Power Surge keys in eligible situations. Ads require an internet connection, never affect scan speed, candidate keys, or puzzle outcomes, and are removed for players with the permanent Continuous Mode Unlock. Where required, the app presents Google's consent choices; you can review them later from Privacy choices in the app menu when that option is available. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Is this mining Bitcoin?
No. Mining secures the Bitcoin network by hashing block headers; our app merely guesses private keys inside predefined ranges to see if any unlock the funded puzzle addresses. This is a cryptographic search over public puzzle ranges, not an industrial crypto mine.
How likely am I to win?
The remaining puzzles are still astronomically hard, but "almost zero" is not the same as zero. Each still-funded range has one private key that unlocks it; the challenge is that the key space is enormous. In a 71-bit range, for example, that means one winning key among about 270 possible keys. Low-bit ranges (e.g. 25 bits) can be replayed for experimenting and learning; high-bit ranges (100+ bits) are purely for the thrill. We highlight the lowest unsolved in the difficulty level picker, your “best bad odds,” so to speak. To estimate your personal chance, we've added the ratio of the number of keys tried by the total number of keys in that range for each puzzle.
How do I claim BTC if I find a winning key?
Congratulations! If you have successfully generated a private key that matches one of the publicly known and still-funded puzzle addresses, you have solved a legendary cryptographic challenge!
Managing private keys and moving cryptocurrency can be risky, so Bitcoin Puzzle Quest does not provide financial, legal, or operational instructions. We do offer a short, informational overview to explain the general concepts involved:

How have some high-bit difficulty levels already been solved?New
The puzzle creator(s) left a "small" clue by making an outgoing transaction from those addresses, which reveals the public key. Levels 75,80,85,90,95,100,105,110,115,120,125,130 and 135 have been solved this way. A known public key allows puzzle hunters to use square-root search techniques such as Pollard's kangaroo or baby-step giant-step. For Puzzle 135, that changes the generic work from searching an interval of 2134 candidates to roughly 267 curve operations, before implementation-specific factors. The remaining public-key-exposed levels are 140,145,150,155 and 160-bit. See the two search cases explained. The puzzle creator(s) have confirmed that no other clues or backdoors exist.
Why can't BPQ keep scanning in the background?
Your device suspends nearly all CPU-intensive work when an app is backgrounded or the screen locks. Continuous Mode runs only while the app is in the foreground and the device is awake (e.g., charging with Auto-Lock disabled).
What is Continuous Mode and do I need it?
Continuous Mode automates the “Go!” button and adds sequential scanning. You don't need it, manual tapping and batch modes are always free, but it's perfect if you'd rather watch the numbers roll than the DIY “robot finger.” You can get it three ways: earn temporary access for free through Power Surge (Daily Chest + invites), top up with Million-Key Packs, or buy the Continuous Mode Unlock for permanent, unlimited use.

Is Continuous Mode a subscription?
No. Nothing in Bitcoin Puzzle Quest is a subscription. The Continuous Mode Unlock is a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks continuous and sequential scanning on your Apple ID for this app, and the Million-Key Packs are one-off consumable top-ups. Both are subject to Apple's usual App Store rules.
Can Continuous Mode pay for itself if I find a winning key?
In theory, yes. If you ever found a private key for a still-funded puzzle address, the BTC value could be far larger than the cost of Continuous Mode, possibly tens of thousands of times larger.

In practice, you should not buy Continuous Mode as an investment. The remaining puzzle ranges are astronomically large, and the chance of finding a winning key is extremely small. Continuous Mode does not change the odds of any single key. It simply lets you scan more comfortably, continuously, and sequentially without tapping manually.

The best way to think about it: Continuous Mode is an optional upgrade for players who enjoy the hunt and want a more hands-off experience. Only spend what you would be comfortable treating as entertainment, not as financial advice or an expected return.

Continuous Mode is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. If the upgrade button does not work, or the purchase fails, please try the following:
  • Make sure you are signed in with a valid Apple ID and that your payment method is up to date.
  • Check that in-app purchases are allowed in Settings on your device.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection and try again after a few minutes.
  • If the App Store shows a specific error message, follow the link in that message or visit Apple Support and check your purchase history.
If you still cannot complete the purchase, please contact us with screenshots of any error messages so we can help you investigate.
I got a new iPhone. How do I restore my purchase?New
Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Then open Bitcoin Puzzle Quest and tap Restore Purchases on the Continuous Mode unlock screen. This restores the permanent, one-time Continuous Mode Unlock. Power Surge key packs (10M, 50M, 100M, and 250M keys) are consumable purchases, so they cannot be restored with Apple's Restore Purchases feature when you move to a new device.
Is my data private?
Candidate private keys, matches, and found numbers stay on your device. Puzzle and key-attempt counters are also stored locally, except that attempt counts may be sent to Apple if you opt into Game Center leaderboards and achievements. The invite feature exchanges anonymous BPQ codes and eligibility information, including privacy-hardened device or vendor hashes, with Mathricks services, while Google AdMob may process device and ad-interaction data for free-version advertising. Neither service receives your candidate private keys or matches. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Does BPQ use proprietary cryptography?
No. Only open, standard algorithms such as secp256k1 (ECDSA), SHA-256, and RIPEMD-160, as specified by IETF/ISO are utilized to compute Bitcoin addresses from private keys.
Why is my device heating up?
Generating tons of keys per minute taxes the CPU. For marathon runs, keep your device on a charger, use Low Power Mode (it only affects screen refresh), and give it breaks if it gets too warm.
Does BPQ have a mini-game?
Absolutely! Double-tap the treasure-chest icon on the main screen to open the Daily Chest, a quick 3×3 sliding puzzle. It started life as a cheeky Easter egg, but solving it now rewards you with Power Surge keys, a free trial of Continuous Mode, once per day. Because every epic quest deserves a hidden side quest and a daily bonus.

Start with our plain-English Bitcoin Puzzle guide, then use the BPQ puzzle status ledger for verified puzzle data and win-announcement sources. When will this quest end?
Honestly, no one knows and that's part of the fun. The hunt keeps going for as long as the puzzles stay unsolved and the underlying cryptography stays hard to reverse. A few things could change the picture over time:
  • The math holding up. The puzzle rests on the difficulty of working backward from a Bitcoin address to its private key (secp256k1 / ECDSA). As long as that stays computationally infeasible, brute-forcing a high-bit range remains astronomically unlikely, which is why any still-funded unsolved ranges remain difficult to claim.
  • Puzzles getting solved. Hunters do crack ranges over time, especially when a public key is exposed. Each solved level shrinks the pool of remaining challenges, so the quest naturally winds down level by level.
  • Quantum computing. A sufficiently powerful quantum computer running Shor's algorithm could one day derive private keys from exposed public keys far faster than today's hardware. That technology isn't here yet and may be years away, but if it matures it could reshape, or end, this kind of hunt entirely.
  • The creator(s) moving the funds. The Bitcoin in the puzzle addresses belongs to whoever set up the challenge. They are free to withdraw, top up, or rearrange it at any time, which could shrink the prize pool or close the puzzle for good. This is outside our control, Bitcoin Puzzle Quest neither holds nor manages those funds.
Even if the prizes were one day claimed or moved, the app itself doesn't expire: it remains a private, offline playground for exploring cryptographic key-spaces and learning how Bitcoin addresses work.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Game not loading or stuck on the first screen: Force-quit Bitcoin Puzzle Quest, reopen it, and make sure you have enough free storage and a recent iOS version supported by the App Store listing.
  • Game crashes: Restart your device, open BPQ again, and try a smaller difficulty or fewer tries per batch.
  • Continuous Mode stops by itself: Continuous Mode only runs while BPQ is open in the foreground and the screen is on. If you want to keep it running for longer sessions, check that Auto-Lock is disabled or set to a long interval and keep the device awake, for example by leaving it on a charger. Be aware that long continuous runs can warm up your device more quickly, so take breaks if it feels too hot.
  • Device gets hot or battery drains quickly: This is expected when scanning large numbers of keys. Run on a charger if desired, lower the difficulty level or tries per batch, and give the device breaks if it becomes very warm.
  • Cannot purchase or upgrade to Continuous Mode: Check that you are signed in with a valid Apple ID, that in-app purchases are allowed in Settings, and that your payment method is valid. If the App Store shows an error, follow the link in that dialog or contact Apple Support. If you are still stuck, contact us with screenshots.
