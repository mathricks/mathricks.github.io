Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest application for mobile devices and to the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest pages of the Mathricks website. The application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "as is".

Information Collection

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest does not require an account, and Mathricks does not directly ask players for names, email addresses, payment card details, or similar identifying information to use the core game. Candidate private keys, matching results, and found numbers are generated or stored locally and are not sent to Mathricks, Google, or other third parties.

If you contact us by email or through the support form, we receive the contact details and message you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request.

Invite Codes and Game Center

The "Invite Friends" feature uses short, anonymous in-game BPQ codes so players can reward one another with Power Surge keys. These codes are not tied to your name, email address, or contacts. The feature communicates with Mathricks services to register codes, verify eligibility, and deliver rewards, using privacy-hardened hashes of device or vendor identifiers to help enforce eligibility and reward limits. If you choose to join Game Center, your attempt counts may be shared with Apple's Game Center to power leaderboards and achievements, subject to Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Advertising and Consent

The free version of Bitcoin Puzzle Quest may show banner ads, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to support the advertising choices available in your region.

Google's advertising SDKs may process data such as IP address and approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, advertising data, ad interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data. Google may use this information to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, improve reliability, and support advertising analytics. For more information, see Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy.

Depending on your region, consent choices, and the available ad inventory, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, contextual, or limited ads. Where available, you can review or change your choices through Privacy choices in the app menu. Players with the permanent Continuous Mode Unlock do not see ads while that unlock is recognized by the app.

Location Data

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest does not request or collect precise, real-time location information from your device. Google may use an IP address to estimate a general location as part of providing advertising services.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google for advertising, by Apple for Game Center and StoreKit, and by Mathricks services for invite-code rewards and app configuration. These services operate under their own privacy terms. Candidate private keys, matching results, and found numbers are not shared with them.

Website Analytics

The Mathricks website may use analytics and click-measurement tools to understand page visits, app download link clicks, and site performance. These tools are separate from the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest app and are controlled through the website's consent choices where applicable.

Your Choices

  • Where a Google consent message is available, use Privacy choices in the app menu to review or change your advertising choices.
  • You can manage relevant privacy and tracking controls in the iOS Settings app.
  • You can use the permanent Continuous Mode Unlock without ads while that unlock is active.
  • You can contact us with questions about this policy or your privacy choices.

Security

Keeping private-key generation and matching on your device reduces exposure of this sensitive game data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and third-party services process information according to their own security practices.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the application after any update is considered acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the application, contact us at info [at] mathricks [dot] com or via the support page.
