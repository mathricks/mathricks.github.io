Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest application for mobile devices and to the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest pages of the Mathricks website. The application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "as is".

Information Collection

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest does not require an account, and Mathricks does not directly ask players for names, email addresses, payment card details, or similar identifying information to use the core game. Candidate private keys, matching results, and found numbers are generated or stored locally and are not sent to Mathricks, Google, or other third parties.

The core game needs no internet connection at all. Scanning, key generation, and matching run entirely on your device, so you can play in airplane mode and nothing leaves your phone. A connection is only used for optional extras: ads, Game Center leaderboards, invite codes, purchases, and app announcements.

If you contact us by email or through the support form, we receive the contact details and message you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request.

Invite Codes and Game Center

The "Invite Friends" feature uses short in-game BPQ codes so players can reward one another with Power Surge keys. These codes are not tied to your name, email address, or contacts, though they do distinguish one device from another, which is how reward limits are enforced. The feature communicates with Mathricks services to register codes, verify eligibility, and deliver rewards, using privacy-hardened hashes of device or vendor identifiers to help enforce eligibility and reward limits. If you choose to join Game Center, your attempt counts may be shared with Apple's Game Center to power leaderboards and achievements, subject to Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Advertising and Consent

The free version of Bitcoin Puzzle Quest may show banner ads, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to support the advertising choices available in your region.

Google's advertising SDKs may process data such as IP address and approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, advertising data, ad interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data. Google may use this information to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, improve reliability, and support advertising analytics. For more information, see Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy.

Depending on your region, consent choices, and the available ad inventory, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, contextual, or limited ads. Where available, you can review or change your choices through Privacy choices in the app menu. Players with the permanent Continuous Mode Unlock do not see ads while that unlock is recognized by the app.

Apple's Tracking Prompt

On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: Allow "Bitcoin Puzzle Quest" to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites? This request exists solely so that advertising can be personalized. It is not used for scanning, scoring, purchases, leaderboards, invite codes, or any other part of the game.

If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not used, and you will see non-personalized or contextual ads instead. Either way the game itself behaves identically: the same puzzles, the same scan speed, the same purchases, and the same chances. You can change this decision at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Location Data

Bitcoin Puzzle Quest does not request or collect precise, real-time location information from your device. Google may use an IP address to estimate a general location as part of providing advertising services.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google for advertising, by Apple for Game Center and StoreKit, and by Mathricks services for invite-code rewards and app configuration. These services operate under their own privacy terms. Candidate private keys, matching results, and found numbers are not shared with them.

Website Analytics

The Mathricks website may use analytics and click-measurement tools to understand page visits, app download link clicks, and site performance. These tools are separate from the Bitcoin Puzzle Quest app and are controlled through the website's consent choices where applicable.

Your Choices

  • Where a Google consent message is available, use Privacy choices in the app menu to review or change your advertising choices.
  • You can allow or refuse Apple's tracking prompt at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusing it does not change how the game plays.
  • You can use the permanent Continuous Mode Unlock without ads while that unlock is active.
  • You can contact us with questions about this policy or your privacy choices.

Your Rights in the EEA and the UK.

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you specific rights over your personal data.

What we process, and on what basis.

  • Advertising: by including the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the free version, we enable the collection of advertising identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and related diagnostics, and their transmission to Google. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given through the Google consent options and Apple's tracking prompt. If consent is refused where it is required, the advertising SDK is not started and no advertising data is transmitted.
  • What Google does next: Google independently decides how the data it receives is used to select, deliver, measure, and report on ads, and acts as a controller in its own right for that processing. We do not choose which ads you see, receive no profile about you, and cannot access or correct the data Google holds. Rights relating to that processing are exercised directly with Google; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Invite codes and app configuration: privacy-hardened hashes of device or vendor identifiers, used to enforce eligibility and reward limits, plus the app's read-only request for its configuration and advertising policy. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in preventing reward abuse and operating the app reliably (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Support correspondence: the contact details and message you send us. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Game progress, candidate private keys, matching results, and found numbers stay on your device and are never transmitted to us, so they are not part of any processing described above. Deleting the app removes them.

Retention. Invite-code records are kept only as long as needed to enforce reward limits and are then deleted. Support correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your request and to meet any applicable record-keeping obligations. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own published retention practices.

International transfers. Google and Apple may process personal data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Those transfers rely on the safeguards those companies put in place, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision.

Your rights. You have the right to request access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time through Privacy choices in the app menu, or in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

Where to direct a request. Because the app works without an account, we cannot look you up by name or email, and different parts of your data sit with different companies:

  • Invite-code records and support correspondence: these are on our side. Contact us and please include the BPQ code shown in the app: it is the only way we can locate the record that belongs to you, and with it we can confirm what is stored and delete it.
  • Advertising data: held by Google, not by us. Exercise your rights directly with Google through their Privacy Policy. You can also reset or disable your device's advertising identifier in the iOS Settings app at any time.
  • Game Center and purchase history: held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We receive no payment details and cannot alter Game Center records.

If you send us a request and we hold nothing that can be connected to you, we are not required to obtain extra information solely to identify you, and we will tell you so rather than leave you waiting (Art. 11 GDPR). We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live, where you work, or where you believe an infringement occurred.

Security

Keeping private-key generation and matching on your device reduces exposure of this sensitive game data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and third-party services process information according to their own security practices.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the application after any update is considered acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the application, contact us at info [at] mathricks [dot] com or via the support page.
